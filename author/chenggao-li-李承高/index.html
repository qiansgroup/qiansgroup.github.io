Chenggao Li (李承高)

PhD student

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

Chenggao Li is a fourth year Ph.D. student in Computer Science under the supervision of Prof. Qian Zhang at HKUST. His research interests include RIS-assisted RF sensing, smart healthcare and wearable computing. He aims to build efficient, robust and practical systems for ubiquitous IoT applications, whether contact-based or contactless. Currently, he mainly working on smart healthcare with wearable devices.
