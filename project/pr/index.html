Smart Pulmonary Caring

Respiratory, Rehab

Pulmonary rehabilitation is important for individuals with chronic respiratory conditions, such as chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), asthma, pulmonary fibrosis, and others. Pulmonary rehabilitation plays a crucial role in improving the quality of life for individuals with chronic respiratory conditions. It helps individuals regain and maintain their functional abilities, manage symptoms effectively, and enhance their overall well-being. Our group focuses on how to monitor and assess pulmonary rehabilitation via wireless and portable sensing technologies, including Earphone, Speaker, Microphone as well as Depth Camera.
